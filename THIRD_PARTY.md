# Interoperability and dependencies

RevivePlex links to frameworks supplied by Apple on the device and uses the
locally installed Xcode SDK to compile. No Apple firmware or SDK is distributed.
The jailbreak, MobileSubstrate and beigelist are external prerequisites and
are not bundled. Their own licenses and installation instructions apply.

Protocol/API references:
- https://developer.plex.tv/pms/
- https://github.com/pkkid/python-plexapi
- https://github.com/lechium/nitoTV
